Nightreign is an Elden Ring roguelike PvE co-op game where you work with other players to survive.

You have two days to explore, level up, find better gear, and fight bosses. A circle slowly closes in and damages you if you stay outside it.

After surviving the two days, your team fights the Nightlord as the final boss.

My Mods

Better Katana: This Mod makes it so your Executor sword has rot and bleed.

Maris Sword: This Mod makes it so you Spawn with the Maris Sword without lvl requirnments.

Sheath Sword: This Mod makes it so your unsheath Art Of War does damage on sheath.

FreeReign Copy This Mod imitates the FreeReign mod. Practice fighting nightlords.
